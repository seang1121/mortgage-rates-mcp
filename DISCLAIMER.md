# Legal Disclaimer

## Rates Data

Rates shown by this service are **publicly advertised rates** scraped from lender websites. They are **not personalized quotes**.

Actual mortgage rates depend on:
- Credit score
- Loan amount
- Property type and location
- Down payment amount
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Loan-to-value ratio
- Other borrower-specific factors

## Not Financial Advice

This service is provided for **informational purposes only**. It does not constitute financial advice, mortgage advice, or a recommendation to purchase any financial product.

Users should:
- Contact lenders directly for official rate quotes
- Verify all rates before making financial decisions
- Consult with a qualified mortgage professional
- Not rely solely on this data for lending decisions

## Data Accuracy

While we strive for accuracy:
- Rates are scraped from public lender websites and may not reflect the most current offers
- Lender websites may update between our scrape cycles (7:00 AM and 7:00 PM EST daily)
- Some rates may be marked as "stale" if the lender's website was temporarily unavailable
- Rate extraction relies on automated parsing which may occasionally produce errors
- All rates are validated against national benchmarks but are not guaranteed to be error-free

## Regulatory Compliance

This service:
- Does **not** originate, broker, or service mortgage loans
- Does **not** collect personal financial information for lending purposes
- Does **not** sell user data to lenders or third parties
- Is **not** a licensed mortgage broker, lender, or financial advisor
- Aggregates **publicly available** rate information, similar to financial news services

## TILA / RESPA

This service does not trigger Truth in Lending Act (TILA) or Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA) obligations because:
- We do not make credit decisions
- We do not extend credit offers
- We do not collect loan applications
- We aggregate and display publicly posted rate information only
